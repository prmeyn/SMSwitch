# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in SMSwitch (filing issues, reviewing pull requests, commenting or contributing
code) is expected to make that a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions)
and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour through GitHub's **Report content** option, in the `...` menu on the
comment, issue or pull request concerned. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

**Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues.** Report those privately as described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
break this code of conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
